# Taught workflows are household data; recording is browser everywhere, then macOS, then X11

- **Date:** 2026-10-19 (user, plan P3 brief/blueprint)

Workflows learned from a recording (Teach Jarvis, C10 in
[agent/COMPUTER-CONTROL.md](../agent/COMPUTER-CONTROL.md)) live on the household server (cc), as
household data next to routines: owned by the member who taught them, shareable within the
household, and governed by the same rules (C2) as any computer task. Export of public workflows
(Pantry) is not v1 (Q7).

Desktop recording lands in this order (Q8): browser recording first, through the extension, on
everywhere; then desktop recording on macOS (AX notifications + event tap under the existing
grants); then X11 and Windows. On Wayland, recording is **browser-only** — desktop recording only
through what the portals allow, otherwise not at all.

This settles Q7 and Q8 as the plan recommended.
